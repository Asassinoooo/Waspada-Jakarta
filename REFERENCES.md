## Cloudflare and Neon Free-tier deployment target — reviewed 24 September 2026

- **Cloudflare Workers.** [Limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/limits/) and [pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/). Reviewed 24 September 2026. The Workers Free plan documents 100,000 requests/day, 10 ms CPU per HTTP/Cron/Workflow invocation, 128 MB memory, 50 subrequests/request, six simultaneous outbound connections, and at most five Cron triggers/account. - **Cloudflare Hyperdrive PostgreSQL drivers.** [Connect PostgreSQL](https://developers.cloudflare.com/hyperdrive/examples/connect-to-postgres/) and [node-postgres driver](https://developers.cloudflare.com/hyperdrive/examples/connect-to-postgres/postgres-drivers-and-libraries/node-postgres/). Reviewed 27 September 2026. Cloudflare recommends node-postgres for Workers + Hyperdrive and documents the supported minimum version and runtime requirements; this informs ADR-022, but does not prove deployed project compatibility.
- **Cloudflare Workers AI.** [Pricing and free allocation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/pricing/). Reviewed 24 September 2026. Documents a 10,000-Neuron/day Free allocation; some models require a paid Workers plan. Reviewed 24 September 2026; source page updated 23 September 2026. Quota exhaustion suspends compute; Neon Free alone is not a 30-day external backup.
- **Neon Postgres extensions.** [Popular extensions including PostGIS and pgvector](https://neon.com/blog/ten-most-popular-postgres-extensions). Reviewed 24 September 2026. Supports choosing these extensions for the storage design; actual extension setup and workload testing remain part of the later database task.
