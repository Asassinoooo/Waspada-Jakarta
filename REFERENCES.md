## Cloudflare and Neon Free-tier deployment target — reviewed 24 September 2026

- **Cloudflare Workers.** [Limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/limits/) and [pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/). Reviewed 24 September 2026. The Workers Free plan documents 100,000 requests/day, 10 ms CPU per HTTP/Cron/Workflow invocation, 128 MB memory, 50 subrequests/request, six simultaneous outbound connections, and at most five Cron triggers/account. Cloudflare Workflows Free documents 3,000 steps/day and 1 GB-month storage; Hyperdrive Free documents 100,000 SQL statements/day. Quota exhaustion fails operations; these are ceilings, not performance guarantees.
- **Cloudflare static assets.** [Workers static asset billing and limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/static-assets/billing-and-limitations/). Reviewed 24 September 2026. Static asset requests are free and unlimited; requests invoking the API Worker count toward its Free quota.
- **Cloudflare database integration.** [Connect Neon to Workers with Hyperdrive](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/databases/third-party-integrations/neon/). Reviewed 24 September 2026. Documents Neon access through Hyperdrive and use of PostgreSQL drivers from Workers.
- **Cloudflare Workers AI.** [Pricing and free allocation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/pricing/). Reviewed 24 September 2026. Documents a 10,000-Neuron/day Free allocation; some models require a paid Workers plan. Model access, Indonesian task quality and quota behavior remain unverified for this project; no paid fallback is planned.
- **Neon.** [Free plan limits and quotas](https://github.com/neondatabase/website/blob/main/content/faqs/free-plan-limits-and-quotas.md). Reviewed 24 September 2026; source page updated 23 September 2026. Documents 100 CU-hours/project/month, 0.5 GB storage/project, 5 GB public transfer/project/month, scale-to-zero after five minutes, up to 2 CU, instant restore limited to six hours or 1 GB of changes, and one manual snapshot. Quota exhaustion suspends compute; Neon Free alone is not a 30-day external backup.
- **Neon Postgres extensions.** [Popular extensions including PostGIS and pgvector](https://neon.com/blog/ten-most-popular-postgres-extensions). Reviewed 24 September 2026. Supports choosing these extensions for the storage design; actual extension setup and workload testing remain part of the later database task.

## PLATFORM-01 provider source cross-check — accessed 24 September 2026

- **Cloudflare Workers.** [Current limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/limits/) (last updated 5 September 2026) and [pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/) (last updated 28 August 2026). Confirmed request, CPU, memory, subrequest, connection, Cron, and 64 MiB uncompressed bundle limits. [Static asset billing and limitations](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/static-assets/billing-and-limitations/) (last updated 23 April 2026) confirms static asset requests are free and unlimited when served as assets.
- **Cloudflare Workflows.** [Limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workflows/reference/limits/) and [pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workflows/reference/pricing/) (both last updated 21 September 2026). Confirmed 3,000 Free steps/day, 1,024 steps per Free instance, 1 GB-month state, 100,000 executions/day shared with Workers, 10 ms CPU per Free step, and that retries/rollback handlers do not count toward billed steps. Step and state billing took effect 10 August 2026. [Cron Triggers](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/configuration/cron-triggers/) (last updated 4 September 2026) documents UTC schedules and scheduled Worker behavior.
- **Cloudflare Hyperdrive.** [Pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/hyperdrive/platform/pricing/) (last updated 18 June 2026) confirms 100,000 SQL statements/day on Free and that query caching does not exempt statements from the count. [Connection pooling](https://developers.cloudflare.com/hyperdrive/concepts/connection-pooling/) documents transaction-mode pooling and session reset behavior.
- **Cloudflare Workers AI.** [Pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/pricing/) (last updated 17 September 2026), [limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/limits/) and [errors](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/errors/) (last updated 17 September 2026), and the [model catalog](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/models/) (last updated 12 August 2026). - **Neon Free limits.** [Plans](https://github.com/neondatabase/website/blob/main/content/docs/introduction/plans.md) (source page updated 17 September 2026) and [Free plan limits and quotas](https://github.com/neondatabase/website/blob/main/content/faqs/free-plan-limits-and-quotas.md) (updated 23 September 2026). Confirmed 100 CU-hours/project/month, 0.5 GB storage, 5 GB public transfer, maximum 2 CU, five-minute scale-to-zero, six-hour/1 GB restore history, and one manual snapshot. The older [August 2025 pricing article](https://neon.com/blog/new-usage-based-pricing) listed 50 CU-hours; Neon’s [November 2025 update](https://neon.com/blog/major-compute-price-reduction-on-neon) says the Free compute allowance doubled to 100. Current plans/FAQ were used.
- **Neon extensions.** [Postgres extension matrix](https://github.com/neondatabase/website/blob/main/content/docs/extensions/pg-extensions.md), [PostGIS guide](https://github.com/neondatabase/website/blob/main/content/docs/extensions/postgis.md), and [pgvector guide](https://github.com/neondatabase/website/blob/main/content/docs/extensions/pgvector.md).
