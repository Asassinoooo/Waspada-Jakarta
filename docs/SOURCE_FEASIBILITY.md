**Baseline:** 24 September 2026; latest rights/catalog review 8 October 2026. **Status:** SPEC-01 design accepted by root review; provider reuse approvals remain open. BMKG's nowcast documentation publishes an API and a 60-requests-per-minute-per-IP ceiling, while its general terms require written permission for third-party-app integration; no Waspada permission or storage/model-processing terms are recorded. PetaBencana publishes a non-commercial CC BY-NC 4.0 user license, but the project's deployment, API, retention, and model-processing fit remains unconfirmed. ANTARA's RSS and current terms do not settle Waspada's server-side storage, AI-processing, and public-display scope. Satu Data Jakarta marks the CRM aggregate dataset open; the crime dataset's metadata and dataset-specific terms remain unverified. **Scope:** source onboarding and retention policy for the prototype, not implemented collectors.

Public reachability proves only that an endpoint responded. It does not grant permission to archive, transform, or republish its content. Each source stays disabled for automated acquisition until its owner, remit, access conditions, attribution, reuse rights, refresh limits, and deletion obligations are recorded in the source registry. When a right is unknown, use a labelled fixture; a moderator may record a source URL and review outcome, but must not persist source text, excerpts, or model-derived content until the applicable terms allow it. The terms review below is a source-feasibility reading, not a legal determination, and does not activate any connector. No live collector is implemented.

## Cross-source failure and freshness rules

Store `fetched_at` separately from source publication, observation/event, and explicit validity timestamps. Preserve provider time zones. Use `Retry-After` when present, bounded exponential backoff with jitter otherwise, content-type/size checks, and allowlisted redirects. Record last success, last attempted fetch, status/error class, and parser version. Three consecutive failed scheduled polls raise a connector-health alert; successful fetches, `304`s, empty feeds, or a newly built feed do not renew evidence freshness. Do not convert coverage gaps to all-clear states. A correction, retraction, or source revocation invalidates dependent excerpts, chunks, vectors, caches, and claims; remove or review any public claim that no longer has eligible support.

## Proposed retention schedule

These are conservative prototype defaults, subordinate to shorter provider terms, privacy obligations, or a deletion request. A source with unknown rights has **zero retained raw content, excerpts, or vectors** until its permission basis is recorded. This schedule is not a legal determination.

| Record | Proposed maximum | Expiry and deletion behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source registry and acquisition-health metadata | Approval review at least every 12 months; retain until 24 months after disablement, then delete or re-approve. | Keep only source identity/domain, access/permission decision, poll health, and review dates. Do not put feed/article bodies in logs. |
| Evidence provenance, canonical URL/provider ID, timestamps, content hash, attribution, reviewer decision, and source-to-claim links | 24 months after the last related publication/review. | Keep only fields permitted by source terms. If rights expire earlier, remove content-derived fields and retain a minimal non-content audit tombstone if allowed. |
| Permitted supporting text excerpts and extracted text | While the related event version is current, plus 90 days; hard cap 365 days from fetch. | Shorter source terms win. On correction, expiry, or revocation, remove the superseded text unless a documented right allows historical retention. |
| Raw HTML, XML, PDF, image or other fetched file | No durable copy by default. If permission allows temporary disk use: delete after parsing, with a 24-hour maximum; parser-failure quarantine is capped at 7 days. | Keep outside Git and backups where possible. No source images/video or full-article mirror without explicit permission. Record only an allowed hash and fetch result. |
| Chunks, embeddings/vectors, derived summaries and caches | Same expiry as their permitted source text. | Treat vectors as source-derived content. On deletion/retraction, invalidate serving immediately and purge primary/index/cache copies within 24 hours; verify tombstones on restore. |
| Moderator/publication audit trail | 24 months after action. | Retain actor ID, action, record/version IDs, decision reason and timestamps; exclude source bodies, excerpts and unnecessary personal data. If a source-specific right is shorter, keep only the non-content fields allowed. |
| Encrypted external backups | Target: maximum 30-day retention, only after a no-cost export/storage path is designed and tested. | Neon Free alone provides up to six hours or 1 GB of instant-restore history and one manual snapshot, not a 30-day off-provider backup. Until a free export and deletion-replay rehearsal passes, use synthetic/historical demo data only and do not activate live-source persistence. Backup provider, region and deletion guarantees remain open. |

Deletion cascade: source revision/raw object → extracted text and excerpts → evidence chunks and vectors → caches/index snapshots → dependent draft/public claim and summaries → fixture/evaluation copies if they contain real restricted content. Reassess and withdraw or qualify a published claim if support is removed; deletion never marks an incident resolved. Keep only the minimum deletion/audit tombstone allowed by the applicable terms. Synthetic demo fixtures stay namespaced and visibly labelled; real source material in evaluation fixtures follows the same retention and permissions.
